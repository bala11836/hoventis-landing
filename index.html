The ₹32,000 Crore Broken Industry

A massive market
running on phone calls.

India's packaged water market is projected to hit $9.2B by 2032 — yet the 20-litre can delivery segment still operates like it's 2005.

70%

Unorganized & Untracked

Over 70% of India's water can market is controlled by local unorganized vendors with zero digital infrastructure. No order tracking. No payment records. No quality assurance. Thousands of unregistered bottling plants operate without proper RO/UV filtration.

Source: IMARC Group, Persistence Market Research
₹0

Zero Tech Adoption

Suppliers manage 100+ daily deliveries using paper registers and WhatsApp groups. No demand forecasting. No route optimization. Manual billing causes payment disputes worth lakhs annually. Every water supplier is one spreadsheet crash away from chaos.

Source: Industry interviews & market analysis

Customer Trust Crisis

Counterfeit water cans are 3x more common than genuine branded ones in tier-2 cities. FSSAI classifies packaged water as "high-risk food." Consumers have no way to verify water quality, supplier credentials, or hygiene compliance before they drink.
