The Problem

A $3.7 billion industry
running on phone calls.

India's 20-litre water can market serves over 150 million households. Yet 70% of it operates without any digital infrastructure.

70%

Unorganized Market

The majority of India's water can market is controlled by local vendors with no digital tools. Thousands of unregistered bottling plants operate without proper RO/UV filtration, making water quality unverifiable for consumers.

Zero

Digital Infrastructure

Suppliers manage hundreds of daily deliveries using paper registers and WhatsApp. No order tracking, no demand forecasting, no automated billing. Payment disputes run into lakhs annually due to manual record-keeping.

3x

Counterfeit Risk

Counterfeit water cans outnumber genuine ones in tier-2 and tier-3 cities. FSSAI classifies packaged water as a "high-risk food category" under IS 14543. Consumers have no way to verify quality or supplier credentials.

Product

WaterTins

A unified platform covering every stakeholder in the water delivery chain — customers, suppliers, delivery staff, and platform administrators.

Customers

Order in seconds, not phone calls

Discover nearby suppliers, browse water products, choose 2-hour delivery windows, track orders in real-time, set up recurring subscriptions, and pay digitally through UPI, cards, or net banking.

Suppliers

Run your business, not spreadsheets

Manage product catalogs with configurable pricing, accept and assign orders, onboard delivery staff, track revenue and commissions, handle customer deposits, and access monthly business analytics.

Delivery

Optimized routes, zero confusion

View assigned deliveries with customer locations on a map, update delivery status in real-time, manage daily routes, and handle empty can returns — all from a single interface.

Administration

Complete platform oversight

Monitor platform-wide analytics, manage supplier onboarding and verification, configure commission structures, track settlements, and ensure quality compliance across all operations.

Customers pick 2-hour delivery slots. Suppliers manage capacity per slot to prevent over-promising and ensure on-time delivery.

Digital Payments

Recurring Subscriptions
