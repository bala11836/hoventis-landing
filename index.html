The Problem

Water delivery in India is still manual

Millions of households rely on water can delivery, yet the entire process runs on phone calls, cash, and guesswork.

📞

Phone-Based Ordering

Customers still call suppliers to place orders. No tracking, no confirmation, no visibility.

💸

Cash-Only Payments

Suppliers lose track of payments. Monthly billing is error-prone and creates disputes.

📋

Zero Analytics

Suppliers can't predict demand, track deliveries, or optimize routes. Everything is manual.

AI Integration

Powered by AI for smarter operations

We're integrating AI models to automate customer support, predict demand, and enable natural language ordering.

Automated chatbot that handles order queries, delivery updates, complaints, and FAQs via in-app chat — reducing support load by 80%.

📈 Smart Demand Forecasting
